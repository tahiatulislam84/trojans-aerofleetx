PRIVACY POLICY

Local-first by design

NO ACCOUNT

Data collection: This Research Edition does not transmit personal information, maintenance records, camera images, audio, or usage analytics to TROJANS servers. The application has no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, login system, or cloud account.

Device processing: Camera, optional microphone, selected images, inspection evidence, maintenance records, and training results are processed locally on the device. Android permission controls remain available in system settings.

Storage and deletion: Demonstration records are kept in local WebView storage. Exports are created only when the user requests them. Use “Erase all local app data” below or clear the app’s storage in Android Settings to delete local records.

Research limits: Do not enter confidential airline, passenger, employee, or controlled technical data into this public demonstration build.
